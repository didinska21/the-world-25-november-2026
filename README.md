# THE WORLD — Wednesday 25 November 2026

One day of world news, one picture: a full newspaper front page for Wednesday
25 November 2026, drawn entirely in code (Python + PIL) so every headline
stays perfectly legible.

**44 date-checked stories · 9 regions — every one happened or was first
reported on this Wednesday.**

## The leads

- Houthis hit Riyadh's tallest tower — 11 wounded in the King Abdullah
  Financial District (Middle East)
- Shenzhou-24 lifts off with the first Pakistani in orbit, bound for
  Tiangong station (East Asia)
- CIA and the top US spy office hit by cyberattack (North America)
- Russian strike kills two in Odesa; Kyiv drones set a block ablaze (Europe)
- Huawei guilty of stealing trade secrets — 10 of 12 counts (East Asia)
- 30 hostages seized in a Cartagena supermarket (Latin America)

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) copies the bundled `entry.png`
to the output directory, byte for byte, on every run.

## How it was made

The front page is drawn, not photographed: six lead panels with hand-drawn
scenes (Riyadh skyline, Shenzhou-24 on the pad, server room, burning block,
microchip, supermarket), 38 brief cards, a nine-region colour key, a UTC
timeline strip, a world pin map, and a date footer. Every story was checked
against a report dated Wednesday 25 November 2026 before it made the page —
no story from an earlier day was reused.

Story count by region: North America 8, Europe 9, Middle East 7, Africa 4,
South Asia 4, East Asia 6, Southeast Asia 2, Oceania 3, Latin America 1 —
44 stories in total across 9 regions.
